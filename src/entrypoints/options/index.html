Auto-translation

New messages in the WhatsApp Chats you choose, the Translated Chats, are translated into your language and sent to your Telegram bot. To answer, reply (swipe left) to a message in Telegram: it is translated into that Chat's language and sent straight away. WhatsApp Web must stay open in a Chrome tab (it can be in the background); it switches to a Translated Chat when a message comes in or a reply goes out.

    4. Voice messages

    Voice notes are turned into text by a speech-to-text service with your own API key (OpenAI-compatible, such as OpenAI or Groq), then translated like any message. Both ways: voice notes from a Translated Chat arrive in Telegram as text, and voice notes you send the bot reach the Chat as translated text. Without a key, voice notes are only announced. The audio goes to that service.
